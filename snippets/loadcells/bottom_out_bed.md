!!! danger "Bottoming out your bed"

    If you are using filament spools to get to that magical 6kg you might end up moving your bed all the way to the bottom of the gantry, please be aware that if you do this there is a chance
    belts will skip when you try to next home the bed as homing does a 3mm z-hop even as part of homing X and Y, this is intentional to avoid nozzle scraping the bed.   The first time you home
    the printer after bottom it out its likely to be safe because Simple AF actually reduces the max z height by 3mm for this specific reason, however if you end up homing the printer twice
    while its bottomed out, the second time the bed is going to hit the bottom of the chamber and this might cause damage but at least its very likely to result in a skipped belt.  To avoid
    this if you think your bed is all the way at the bottom be sure to manually move it up a few mm befor homing again by manually turning a lead screw.
