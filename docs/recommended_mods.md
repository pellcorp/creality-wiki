# Recommended Mods

!!! warning

    These are third party mods, there **is absolutely no support available** for them via the Simple AF discord.

## K1 Series

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Motherboard Fan Mount MK2__

    ---

    Great for reducing overheating on the bottom of the printer.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/540070-motherboard-fan-mount-mk2-collection-creality-k1-s)

-   __K1 Linear Rail Gantry__

    ---

    Linear Rail Gantry Conversion Mod for Creality K1 Series Printers.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: GitHub](https://github.com/pellcorp/K1-Linear-Rail-Gantry)

</div>

## Ender 3 V3 SE / KE

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Rear Rail Mount (Y Rail Mod)__

    ---

    Print the rear rail mount in a single piece.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/721192-rear-rail-mount-for-ender-3-v3-seke-y-rail-mod)

-   __Simple Gantry Support (Z Brace)__

    ---

    Dampens the gantry shaking.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/817583-simple-gantry-support-metricinch-z-brace-for-ender)

-   __SE & KE Y Rails Conversion__

    ---

    Y rails for the bed, for MGN9H or MGN12H rails, the SE needs the alternate rail adapter.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: GitHub](https://github.com/pellcorp/Creality-Ender-3-V3-SE-KE/tree/main/SE%2BKE%20Y%20Rails%20Conversion)

-   __SE & KE Z Belt Tensioner__

    ---

    A simple tensioner for the Z belt.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: GitHub](https://github.com/pellcorp/Creality-Ender-3-V3-SE-KE/tree/main/SE%2BKE%20Z%20Belt%20Tensioner)

-   __KE Beacon/Cartographer Mount__

    ---

    Simple mount for a Beacon or Cartographer 3D probe, must be used with a right angle probe.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: GitHub](https://github.com/pellcorp/Creality-Ender-3-V3-SE-KE/tree/main/KE%20Beacon-Cartographer%20Mount)

-   __KE Quick Release Accelerometer Mounts__

    ---

    Quick release mounts for the Creality and Fysetc accelerometers.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: GitHub](https://github.com/pellcorp/Creality-Ender-3-V3-SE-KE/tree/main/KE%20Quick%20Release%20Accelerometer%20Mounts)

</div>
