## K1 Community Projects

A lot of the groundwork for running open klipper on the K1 Series was done by others:

- [Creality Helper Script](https://github.com/Guilouz/Creality-Helper-Script) and [Creality K1 Extracted Firmwares](https://github.com/Guilouz/Creality-K1-Extracted-Firmwares) by Guilouz
- [GuppyScreen](https://github.com/ballaswag/guppyscreen), [K1 Klipper Mod](https://github.com/ballaswag/creality_k1_klipper_mod) and [K1 Discovery](https://github.com/ballaswag/k1-discovery) by ballaswag
- [K1 Klipper installer](https://github.com/K1-Klipper/installer_script_k1_and_max) for the original sensorless homing config
- [OpenK1](http://openk1.org) for the original webcam service
